Day 5 — Aruba!

⚡ Thor says:
"Hey Monty! Nanny and Grandad have arrived in Aruba! 🏖️ This island is famous for having some of the most beautiful beaches in the WHOLE WORLD! The sand is so white and soft it feels like walking on clouds! The water is the brightest blue you've ever seen — even brighter than my lightning! And there are special trees called Divi-Divi trees that lean sideways because the wind blows them all day long. They look like they're doing a funny dance!"
🕷️ Spider-Man reports:
"I've been exploring Aruba and WOW! The beaches here are AMAZING! Eagle Beach is one of the best beaches on planet Earth! The sand is softer than your bed at home! And guess what? The water is so calm and clear you can see right to the bottom where little colourful fish are swimming! There are also cool rock formations called the Natural Bridge and the California Lighthouse where you can see the whole island!"
🦾 What they're doing today!
Morning Adventure: Right now they're at one of Aruba's world-famous beaches — probably Eagle Beach or Palm Beach! The sand is super white and soft, and the water is warm and calm like a giant bathtub! They're walking along the beach, collecting pretty shells, and watching the Divi-Divi trees lean over in the breeze. They might even see some tropical fish in the clear water!

Lunchtime: They're eating lunch at a beach restaurant with their toes in the sand! They're trying fresh seafood and yummy tropical fruit smoothies. Maybe they're eating 'keshi yena' (cheese with meat) or fresh grilled fish with rice and plantains. It's so sunny they're wearing sunglasses and sun cream!

Afternoon Fun: This afternoon they might visit the California Lighthouse to see the whole island from up high, or go shopping in Oranjestad (the capital city) for souvenirs! They're definitely buying you something special — maybe a t-shirt with a Divi-Divi tree on it, or a little wooden lighthouse! They're also taking LOTS of photos of the beautiful blue water and white sandy beaches to show you!

Special Note: Aruba is called "One Happy Island" because everyone who visits has such a brilliant time! The sun shines almost EVERY day — it hardly ever rains! That's why the beaches are perfect for playing and swimming! 🌞

Barbados 🇧🇧

Sat 7 February — Embark Day!

A beautiful tropical island where Nanny & Grandad got on the big ship! It has amazing beaches with soft white sand and friendly green monkeys that live in the trees!

✅ Day 3 — Visited!
🦩

Bonaire 🇧🇶

Mon 9 February

Bonaire is famous for its beautiful pink flamingos! The water here is so clear you can see all the way to the bottom. It's one of the best places in the whole world to see colourful fish!

⚡ Thor Fun Fact: Bonaire has THOUSANDS of pink flamingos! They stand on one leg — even I can't do that in battle!
✅ Day 4 — Visited!
🏘️

Curaçao 🇨🇼

Tue 10 February

Curaçao has the most colourful buildings you've ever seen! Every house is painted a different bright colour — pink, yellow, blue, orange! The whole town looks like a rainbow!

🦾 Iron Man Fun Fact: The colourful buildings in Willemstad are so bright, I could spot them from my suit flying high above!
📍 Day 5 — TODAY!
🏖️

Aruba 🇦🇼

Wed 11 February

Aruba has some of the prettiest beaches in the whole world! The sand is super soft and white, and the water is bright blue. They also have cool Divi-Divi trees that lean sideways because of the wind!

🕷️ Spider-Man Fun Fact: The Divi-Divi trees here bend sideways in the wind — like me dodging bad guys!
Day 7
🏄

Jamaica 🇯🇲

Fri 13 February

Jamaica has amazing waterfalls you can actually climb up! It's called Dunn's River Falls and the water splashes all over you! Jamaica is also where super-fast runners come from!

⚡ Thor Fun Fact: Dunn's River Falls crashes down like my lightning bolts! And Jamaican hummingbirds are as colourful as my cape!
Day 9
🐎

Grand Turk 🇹🇨

Sun 15 February

Grand Turk is a tiny island with the most amazing turquoise water! There are wild horses and donkeys that walk around the island! And the beach has the softest sand ever!

🦾 Iron Man Fun Fact: Wild horses roam free on Grand Turk! Even my suit's GPS can't keep up with them!
Day 11
⚓

Antigua 🇦🇬

Tue 17 February

Antigua has 365 beaches — that's one for every single day of the year! It also has an old harbour where real pirate ships used to dock! How cool is that?!

🕷️ Spider-Man Fun Fact: 365 beaches means a NEW beach every day for a whole year! Even I'd run out of web fluid visiting them all!
Day 12
🦜

Dominica 🇩🇲

Wed 18 February

Dominica is called the "Nature Island" — it's covered in thick rainforest with giant waterfalls and colourful parrots! It even has a BOILING lake where the water bubbles like a giant kettle!

⚡ Thor Fun Fact: Dominica's rainforest is so wild it looks like the forests of Asgard! And the Sisserou parrot is as mighty as my hammer Mjolnir!
